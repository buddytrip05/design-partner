# Discovery call guide: [feature]

**Length:** 45–60 minutes · **Who we're talking to:** [roles] · **What we need to learn:** [ledger IDs, as questions]

**Legend:** **V1** = in this version. **EXPLORING** = an idea, not a commitment. Say which when you show anything.

## 1. Open (5 min)

"Thanks for the time. We're looking for where this is wrong, so blunt is helpful. Nothing here is final."

## 2. How they work today (15 min)

Ask about the last time, not the next time.

- "Walk me through the last time you [job]."
- "Who else touches it? What do they use?"
- "Who logs in, as whom, on what device?"
- "What do you keep up to date, and what gets skipped?"
- "What do you do outside the product to get this done?"

## 3. Assumptions to challenge (10 min)

| Our assumption (ledger ID) | Neutral question | What would change our mind |
|---|---|---|
| [L7: reps keep statuses current] | "How do you use statuses today?" | [They rarely set them] |

Don't ask them to confirm your answer. Ask what they do.

## 4. Show the design (15 min)

- Give a task, not a tour: "You've just come back from a call. Show me what you'd do."
- For each part shown, note: reaction, exact words, and whether it was prompted.
- For EXPLORING items, ask what they'd give up to have it.

**Optional exercises**
- **Card sort:** put these [n] items in the order you'd want to see them.
- **$100 test:** spend $100 across these ideas.

## 5. Close (5 min)

- "What did we not ask that we should have?"
- "If this shipped tomorrow, what would stop your team using it?"
- "Can we come back to you with the next version?"
